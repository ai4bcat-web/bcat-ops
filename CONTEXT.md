## What it is
Internal operations dashboard for BCAT dispatch — calendar scheduling, load management, driver schedules, fleet/equipment registry, live truck tracking, maintenance, maintenance invoices, expense/fuel tracking, insurance premium tracking, weekly fleet profitability, a fleet-manager dashboard (PM/DOT-due tracking plus open Motive engine fault codes), a Fleet Miles page (per-truck weekly odometer miles, MPG and revenue per mile from Motive), finances, a Weekly Cash Check-in (biweekly cash/AR/AP/cards + MTD-profit log per profit center with a 3–12-month cash outlook, factoring scenario and a Slack reminder the morning after each payroll), a Factoring Queue (one row per PRO emailed to ivanfactoring@bcatcorp.com, tracked Need to factor → Pending with OTR → Factored, with a per-row OTR Solutions readiness panel that submits the invoice + POD + rate confirmation to OTR on a human click and mirrors OTR's status back hourly), an appointment-booking board (every shipment with its pickup/delivery booking-workflow status and booking history, plus an Appt Changes worklist and a daily unconfirmed-appointments Slack digest), a Customers & Locations directory (the reusable address book behind the Load form; a Location carries the appointment contact emailed to request appt times), driver pay (Amazon, box-truck, and owner-operator settlements off brokerage loads), a driver PWA at `/driver` (its own Cognito pool: drivers scan rate confirmations/PODs and view their weekly settlement) with a staff Driver Docs page over those submissions, Amazon driver disputes (staff queue plus a public, no-login driver portal at `/amazon-disputes` for filing a dispute and checking the shared status board), email/Slack intake, DOT compliance & driver onboarding, a Files hub (the driver roster plus everything on file per driver/truck, downloadable as one PDF packet), Best Care Auto Transport vehicle-quote and booking-confirmation emailers plus a pricing-margin control for the bestcareautotransport.com quote tool, a Reddit reply queue (marketing), and audit logging. Access is per-page and EXPLICIT: every route is gated by `RequirePage` on Cognito `page-<key>` groups — a user sees only the pages they have been granted, and no grants means no pages at all. The owner account (ryne@bcatcorp.com) and members of the Cognito `ADMIN` group (the Users page "Make admin" toggle) bypass the allowlist and see every page; the legacy admin email allowlist grants feature privileges only, NOT page access.

**Driver pay:** `DriverPayPeriod` (biweekly gross pay, Paychex seam) · `AmazonTrip` (weekly trip-based Amazon pay lines) · `AmazonPayMaster` (archive of uploaded master CSVs, raw file in S3) · `BoxTruckTrip` (biweekly box-truck shipment lines) · `DriverPaySetting` (per-driver pay model: `payGroup` AMAZON | LOCAL | BOX_TRUCK | OWNER_OPERATOR — owner operators settle off brokerage loads, not a trip sheet — expense timing, fuel card, fixed deductions; `rateHistory` pins past rate windows `[{from, until, payPercent, expensesBeforePercent}]` — pay weeks starting in `[from, until)` use that pinned model via `effectivePayRate()` in `src/lib/driverPay.ts`, so changing a driver's current rate never rewrites already-paid weeks; `fixedExpenses` is a list of DATED REVISIONS `[{ label, amount, from, until, revisionId, expenseId, recordedAt/By, endedAt/By }]` — editing a charge ends the old revision and appends a new one rather than rewriting it (`src/lib/fixedExpenseHistory.ts`, edited via `src/features/driver-pay/FixedExpenseEditor.tsx`); a revision may instead carry a `mileage: { costPerMile, miles }` basis — Amazon settings only (`allowMileage`), box-truck stays fixed-amount — in which case `amount` is derived by `calculateMileageExpense()` as cost per mile × hand-entered miles (NOT an ELD/trip-mile feed), rounded to cents exactly once with sub-cent per-mile rates kept, and the statement line reads "N miles × $x/mi = $y"; and `effectiveFixedExpenses(list, periodStart, periodEndInclusive)` prorates every overlapping revision by actual calendar days inside the settlement period, allocating cent rounding per `expenseId` series so a mid-period rate change can't create or lose a cent; partial-period lines carry `proratedDays` and print as "label (N/M days)" on the statement (`fixedExpenseLineLabel`). A revision is shared through the pay split by default (an after-expenses driver bears only their pay % of it); `afterPercent: true` ("Driver pays in full, after the split" in the editor) makes the driver bear the line in full — the pay hooks route those revisions into the statement's debits instead of the pre-split deductions (`fixedDebits` on `useAmazonPay` / `useBoxTruckPay`, printed with the debits on the PDF). Any revision may also carry `companyAmount`, the company's own separate share of the same charge per period (e.g. its half of a truck lease): it never appears on the statement, is prorated by days exactly like `amount`, and is booked as a company expense in Amazon profitability. Delete only for mistakes; a per-week waive button writes an offsetting negative one-off instead) · `DriverPayDeduction` (per-week one-off charges; negative amount = refund, shown green; the form's miles × cost-per-mile mode only computes the amount and bakes the basis into the label — no extra columns) · `DriverPayCredit` (settlement money that didn't come from shipment gross profit, on both Amazon weekly and box-truck settlements; `kind` is `'CREDIT'` — default for null/pre-existing rows, extra pay like detention/layover/bonus/reimbursement added at 100% — or `'DEBIT'` — cash advance/damage/escrow/overpayment subtracted at 100% AFTER the % model runs, so check = net + credits − debits; the pay % is NOT applied to either, `reasonCode` is a plain string so the list can grow without a redeploy — debit reasons now include `LEASE_MILEAGE` (per-mile charge on the leasing company's invoice) and `IFTA` (quarterly fuel tax on a leased truck), both driver-pays-100% items that the fixed-expense editor explicitly steers to a Debit rather than a shared fixed expense; optional `miles` + `costPerMile` record the mileage basis of a per-mile debit (amount = miles × costPerMile via `calculateMileageExpense`, both nullable so legacy rows are untouched) and `creditLineLabel` prints the stored amount alongside it — "Lease mileage — 1234 mi @ $0.18/mi = $222.12" — so the statement/PDF/CSV can never disagree with the check — see `src/lib/payCredits.ts`)

**Disputes:** `AmazonDispute` (driver claims that Amazon underpaid/owes on a trip; Google Form → intake Lambda, source `GOOGLE_FORM`, plus `MANUAL` and `DRIVER_PORTAL` — portal rows are written straight to DynamoDB by `dispute-portal-api` with `id = submissionId` and `externalId = portal:<submissionId>`, so a retried submission never creates a second row; workflow PENDING → POSTED → PAID | REJECTED; `evidence` AWSJSON holds three kinds of file in one array — the driver's read-only `dispute-proofs/` uploads (`CONFIRMATION`/`PHOTO`), staff manual proofs in `dispute-staff-proofs/{id}/` (same kinds, replaceable), and staff `AMAZON_RESPONSE` screenshots in `dispute-responses/{id}/` — split by `src/features/disputes/disputeEvidence.ts` so a response edit can never drop the driver's proof, alongside `amazonResponse`/`amazonResponseAt`/`amazonResponseBy`; `resolvedAmount` is the dollars actually recovered on a PAID row, and `settlementPeriodStart`/`settlementTripId`/`settlementDriverId` link the recovery to the `AmazonTrip` DISPUTE row it was posted as, making a re-save a move/re-price instead of a second payout)

One document dataset: `ComplianceDocument` is the ONE store for every uploaded document/photo (drivers and trucks). Compliance, Onboarding, Asset Documents and the Files hub all read it through the shared cache in `src/lib/complianceDocStore.ts`, which owns the single "which document is current" rule so those pages can't disagree about a doc's status. Document-type keys are shared across `src/lib/complianceRequirements.ts` (driver) and `src/lib/truckDocs.ts` (asset) — renaming one orphans every document already uploaded under it. A driver's file slots are DERIVED from `DRIVER_REQUIREMENTS` (every requirement with `requiresDocument`), so the DQ file, the portal and the onboarding checklist come from one list; only the fleet-specific agreement (employment vs lease) is added on top. Uploading a document in the Files hub also completes the onboarding task it satisfies (`findLinkedTask` in `src/hooks/useFileHub.ts`); a failure there logs rather than failing the upload.

Notable model fields: `Load.driverStatus` (ASSIGNED | EN_ROUTE | ON_SITE | DELIVERED — one driver-reported progression per load, separate from stop-level arrived/departed) with `driverStatusAt`/`By` and `driverStatusLat`/`Lng`/`Place` (the ELD fix at the moment of the tap; the PWA collects no position itself). `Load` stops (and their legacy `pickupAppt*`/`deliveryAppt*` mirror fields) carry appointment windows and types (`appt`/`apptEnd`/`apptType`); stops flagged NEED or missing a time show as open on the `/appts` board, everything else as booked (FCFS and range stops never count as open — see `src/lib/apptQueue.ts`), and the transition INTO NEED fires the `notifyApptNeeded` Slack ping. The booking-workflow state also lives inside the same `stops` JSON (no schema change): `apptStatus` (the ladder step), `apptProofs` (`{request, e2open, email}` screenshot keys — the gates), `apptChangeTo` (the wanted new time on CHANGE NEEDED), `apptMoveRequested`/`apptMoveTaskId` (the NEEDS TO BE MOVED flag + its Dennis task), `apptRequestedFor` (the date/time we asked the facility for, stamped on send/REQUESTED so the chip can say what was requested), `apptCleared` (manually cleared from the `/appts` queue via the per-day Clear — row leaves the working view and stops counting as open whatever its dates), and `apptThreadTs` (the stop's Slack thread); the effective status — including grandfathering of pre-ladder stops and the non-Batory ratecon rule — is computed in `src/lib/apptStatus.ts`. `Driver.onboardingStatus` now includes `ARCHIVED` (candidate set aside, reversible) and `Driver.onboardingTemplateId` selects the phased template. `ComplianceSettings.privateDocumentTypes` (document types only admins may see — pay-term paperwork; presentation-level only, still readable via the API) and `ComplianceSettings.documentResponsibility` (documentType → `OFFICE` | `DRIVER`) let both lists change without a redeploy; the responsibility default comes from the catalog's `driverActionable` flag (`src/lib/fileHub.ts`). `Equipment.lastPmDate`/`lastPmMileage` feed the Fleet Manager dashboard's 25k-mi PM countdown. `Equipment.motiveVehicleNumber` (trucks on the Motive ELD only) is the Motive vehicle number when it differs from `unitNumber` — the ELD carried over from a retired truck keeps reporting under the old number — and the Motive syncs match on it first, then the plain unit number; inactive (`active === false`) trucks never claim a Motive vehicle, so a retired unit can hand its ELD/number to its replacement. `Equipment.fuelCardNumbers` is the source of truth for the EFS card → truck mapping (cards move with the truck, e.g. 00056 went from retired 299 to the leased 423166); the hardcoded map in the fuel-import Lambda and `scripts/ingestFuelReport.mjs` is only a fallback when the Equipment query fails. `MaintenanceInvoice.source` (`EMAIL` from the repairs@ pipeline | `MANUAL`), `status` (`PENDING` | `POSTED` | `ARCHIVED`) and `reviewedBy` drive the invoice review queue — effective state is computed in `src/lib/invoiceStatus.ts`, where a null status plus the `INVOICE_QUEUE_CUTOFF` timestamp keeps legacy and manual invoices POSTED (no back-fill needed) and only PENDING/ARCHIVED are excluded from the dashboard and P&L. `MaintenanceInvoice.externalId` is a hash of the SOURCE document (date + vendor + amount + invoice #, or a `sourceDocumentId` — attachment hash / Message-ID + index — for unnumbered invoices) frozen at ingest and deliberately excluding `equipmentId` — keying on the unit made reviewed/archived invoices reappear in the queue after staff re-assigned them (see `scripts/invoiceDedup.mjs`, whose tri-state `classifyDedup` returns DUPLICATE / AMBIGUOUS / NEW and fails an unnumbered source-identified document closed for manual review when it only matches a pre-`sourceDocumentId` row by content). Emailed invoices are written PENDING by `scripts/ingestMaintenanceInvoice.mjs` with a deterministic record id derived from `externalId` (so concurrent runs race on one DynamoDB key; a create conflict counts as a duplicate only after get-by-id confirms the same `externalId`), candidate emails are discovered/tracked by the durable mailbox ledger `scripts/maintenanceMailboxLedger.py` (keyed by Message-ID, replacing the unreliable IMAP Seen flag), and `scripts/dedupeMaintenanceInvoices.mjs` is the read-only preview / `--apply` archive-only cleanup for the rows the old unit-keyed dedup duplicated (see `Docs/WORKFLOWS.md` → Repair invoice intake). `listMaintenanceInvoices` in `src/lib/apiClient.ts` follows every AppSync `nextToken` page (rejecting rather than showing a partial list on a page error), and every repair-spend figure — the Fleet Manager dashboard's Repair Spend widget, the per-unit spend on `/trucks`, and trailer maintenance in the fleet monthly net — counts only POSTED invoices via `isPosted()`.

## Lambda Functions (`amplify/functions/`)
- **slack-intake-webhook** — receives forwarded Slack/email messages → creates IntakeItem records
- **gmail-task-intake** — called by the Gmail Apps Script bridge for mail to the tasks@ distro → creates an IntakeItem (deduped by Gmail message id) and posts to #intake-ivan Slack
- **factoring-intake** — public Function URL (`FactoringIntakeFunctionUrl` output) called by the Gmail bridge Apps Script (`scripts/factoringEmailBridge.gs`) for mail to ivanfactoring@bcatcorp.com; authenticates with the shared `INTAKE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, extracts the PRO from the subject, and creates a `FactoringItem` (status NEED_TO_FACTOR, `id = proNumber`) with an atomic conditional put — PutItem-only IAM, so re-forwarding a PRO is a no-op and never resets its status; after creating a row it fire-and-forget invokes `otr-actions` (`assemble`) to resolve the Load and pre-fill the OTR payload, non-fatally
- **otr-actions** — custom AppSync mutation (`manageOtr`); every interaction between the factoring queue and OTR Solutions' Carrier TMS v3 API (resolve PRO → Load, assemble payload + readiness, record broker MC on the Customer, broker check, submit invoice + upload POD and rate confirmation from S3, sync status); cents ↔ dollars conversion lives only in `src/lib/otrInvoice.ts`. The same resource file defines **otr-status-sync** (`sync.ts`), an hourly (:05) schedule that mirrors OTR's status onto every non-terminal row holding an `otrInvoiceId`
- **driver-app-api** — public Function URL behind the driver PWA; verifies the driver-pool id token (`aws-jwt-verify`) and resolves the caller's roster record server-side (another driver's submission returns 404). Routes: `/me`, `/settlement/weeks` + `/settlement` (the weekly statement built server-side from AmazonTrip or, for owner operators, delivered Loads, plus deductions/credits/fuel), `/submissions` list/create, `/submissions/:id/pod`, `/submissions/:id/complete`, upload and doc-URL presigns (≤ 15 MB, ≤ 12 pages, JPEG/PNG/WebP/PDF into `driver-docs/`). Completing a rate-con submission posts to Slack #intake-ivan and emails ivanloads@bcatcorp.com via SES, and POD replies thread into the same Slack + email threads. Also serves `/email-intake/prepare` + `/email-intake/commit` (shared-secret, two-phase presigned upload) for the ivanloads@ Gmail bridge (`scripts/loadsEmailBridge.gs`), turning each emailed rate con into an EMAIL-source `DriverSubmission`. Read-only IAM on the roster/pay/load tables; only the two submission tables are writable
- **driver-signup-gate** — PreSignUp trigger on `bcat-driver-pool`; rejects any signup whose email doesn't match an active roster driver with an eligible pay group (`AMAZON` / `OWNER_OPERATOR`, the same `scope.ts` list `driver-app-api` uses)
- **vendor-ap-intake** — secret-authenticated Function URL for the vendorpayments@bcatcorp.com Gmail bridge (`scripts/vendorApEmailBridge.gs`; shared `INTAKE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`); two-phase protocol to stay under the 6 MiB Function URL ceiling — `prepare` returns presigned S3 PUT URLs for each original attachment, `commit` HEADs every uploaded key and conditionally creates the `VendorPayable` row
- **vendor-ap-actions** — custom AppSync mutation (`manageVendorPayable`); validates payment details and writes `VendorPayable` + the linked `MaintenanceInvoice` together in one DynamoDB transaction, resolving the caller's email from Cognito
- **pod-actions** — custom AppSync mutation (`managePods`: status / configure / list / backfill / sync / assets / assign / retry / senderMappings / setSenderMapping) AND EventBridge target (every 15 min, `action: 'backfillSchedule'`; deliberately left enabled under `BCAT_ISOLATED_PREVIEW` since it only reads JobsDone and writes the stack's own tables). Stores the JobsDone key + client ID as a SecureString SSM parameter `/bcat/pods/<userPoolId>/connection`, walks the last 7 days of the message feed over JobsDone's WebSocket server-side (every cursor page inside one invocation — the `clientId-index` has no sort key so page order is arbitrary; 50 rows per page because JobsDone answers in one 128 KB API Gateway frame), dedupes attachments by a stable hash, self-invokes asynchronously to archive the original and produce the scanned copy (`scan.ts`: Jimp + tesseract.js from the `pod-scan` Lambda layer built at synth in `amplify/podScanner.ts`, no CDN), returns short-lived presigned S3 URLs, and enforces owner/`ADMIN`/`page-pods` plus a transactional Load existence check and version CAS on assignment

## Intake Pipeline
Forwarded messages (Slack channel posts, plus legacy Gmail) → **slack-intake-webhook** Lambda Function URL → `IntakeItem` created with status `NEW` (source `IVAN_CARTAGE` / `BCAT_LOGISTICS`, `externalSource` `slack`|`gmail`, deduped by `externalId`) → appears in the `/intake` queue. Status changes fire `notifySlackStatusChange` → **slack-status-notifier** posts back to Slack.

### Loads are built by hand — never automatically
A tender arriving at `ivanloads@bcatcorp.com` must NEVER create a `Load`, and must
never place anything on the calendar. The intake path's only job is to capture the
documents and notify Slack; dispatch then builds the load manually once a human has
seen it. The one thing that may happen automatically is the load's details reaching
the owner-operator settlement.

A backfill script (`scripts/backfillIvanloadsLoads.mjs`, removed 2026-10-01) violated
this: it read the ivanloads mailbox and wrote `Load` rows with pickup and delivery
appointments, which put two shipments straight onto the calendar. The two rows it
created (`ivanloads-409670`, `ivanloads-98553`) were moved to Unscheduled rather than
deleted, so their driver and rate still reach the settlement. Do not reintroduce a
script of this shape — the manual step is the process, not a gap to be filled.

## DOT Compliance & Onboarding
The standalone `/compliance` pages are retired — staff manage driver/truck compliance from the Files hub (`/files`), and the company-wide controls (escalation rules, email kill switches, private/responsibility document lists) live at `/settings`. Drivers are invited via `OnboardingInvite` (crypto-random token, ~14-day expiry) and complete the 49 CFR 391.21 application + document uploads in the public `/onboard/:token` portal (served by `onboarding-portal-api`, no Cognito). The driver requirement catalog (`src/lib/complianceRequirements.ts`) covers the DOT Driver Qualification file (49 CFR 391.51), including the annual certification of violations (391.27) and the conditional SPE / medical-variance certificate (391.49); the completed employment application is itself a retained document. Action-only items (Clearinghouse queries, National Registry check, previous-employer inquiry) stay on the checklist and off the file. The `compliance-scanner` cron tracks expirations and raises `ComplianceAlert`s; `EscalationRule`/`EscalationEmailLog`/`ComplianceSettings` govern email escalation (default PAUSED). Never store full SSNs (last 4 only) or full fuel card numbers (last 4 only).
