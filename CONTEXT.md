## What it is
Internal operations dashboard for BCAT dispatch — a Dispatch inbox (Twilio texting/calling with drivers from one number, mirrored both ways into a Slack channel per driver, with primary/backup dispatchers nudged by Slack DM when a text goes unanswered), calendar scheduling, load management, driver schedules, fleet/equipment registry, live truck tracking (a Driver info page with each truck's position, driver, load and copy-for-broker block, plus permanent public per-truck tracking links at `/track/:code`), a dashboard driver leaderboard, maintenance, maintenance invoices, expense/fuel tracking, insurance premium tracking, weekly fleet profitability, a fleet-manager dashboard (PM/DOT-due tracking and open Motive engine fault codes), per-truck weekly MPG from Motive on the fuel page, finances (plus a Profit Review of the Ivan fleet's month-by-month economics, including loaded-vs-empty miles from odometer readings at every driver tap), a Weekly Cash Check-in (biweekly cash/AR/AP/cards + MTD-profit log per profit center with a 3–12-month cash outlook, factoring scenario and a Slack reminder the morning after each payroll), a Factoring Queue (one row per PRO emailed to ivanfactoring@bcatcorp.com, tracked Need to factor → Pending with OTR → Factored, with a per-row OTR Solutions readiness panel that submits the invoice + POD + rate confirmation to OTR's production v2 API on a human click and mirrors OTR's status back hourly; a No Buy broker moves the row to a manual-invoice checklist), a PODs review queue (every POD sent from the driver apps is read by Claude against the load's rate confirmation, then approved or sent back to the driver by a person), an appointment-booking board (every shipment with its pickup/delivery booking-workflow status and booking history, plus a daily unconfirmed-appointments Slack digest), a Customers & Locations directory (the reusable address book behind the Load form; a Location carries the appointment contact emailed to request appt times), driver pay (Amazon, box-truck, and owner-operator settlements off brokerage loads), a driver PWA at `/driver` (its own Cognito pool: owner operators/Amazon drivers get a settlement-first app where they see their weekly settlement and send PODs/rate confirmations per load, or unattached and linked later; Ivan's own LOCAL drivers pick their truck for the day and get a money-free Dashboard with today's pickups/deliveries (on-site/departed taps stamped on the load, delivery ETA, detention flag, POD and stop photos), POD legibility, a daily ELD-logs-needed verdict with their Motive duty log, their truck's next PM, plus an Hours time clock) with a staff Ivan Driver App page and an Employee Hours page (fortnightly time cards with signed corrections), Amazon driver disputes (staff queue plus a public, no-login driver portal at `/amazon-disputes` for filing a dispute and checking the shared status board), email/Slack intake (with tender attachments kept, load prefill from the tender, and an automatic Slack-thread reconcile once the load exists), a 2FA relay (Google Voice verification-code texts → Slack #2fa), DOT compliance & driver onboarding, a Files hub (the driver roster plus everything on file per driver/truck, downloadable as one PDF packet), Best Care Auto Transport vehicle-quote and booking-confirmation emailers plus a pricing-margin control for the bestcareautotransport.com quote tool, a Reddit reply queue (marketing), and audit logging. On the `dev` branch an MVMNT-style TMS (the Aljex replacement) is being built step by step on top of the existing Load: Customer v2 (Step A — status ladder, credit controls, contacts/billing/quoting defaults), Shipment / Order / Load with a shipments board and draft editor (Step B), an outside-carrier directory with booking gates, quick pay and e-signed rate confirmations (Step C), tracking and check calls with a carrier ping link and a public Customer Link (Step D), and — uncommitted in the working tree — shipment documents and delivery (Step E: typed documents, a carrier upload portal, milestone e-mails, POD requests). Access is per-page and EXPLICIT: every route is gated by `RequirePage` on Cognito `page-<key>` groups — a user sees only the pages they have been granted, and no grants means no pages at all. The owner account (ryne@bcatcorp.com) and members of the Cognito `ADMIN` group (the Users page "Make admin" toggle) bypass the allowlist and see every page; the legacy admin email allowlist grants feature privileges only, NOT page access.

## Intake Pipeline
Forwarded messages (Slack channel posts, plus legacy Gmail) → **slack-intake-webhook** Lambda Function URL → `IntakeItem` created with status `NEW` (source `IVAN_CARTAGE` / `BCAT_LOGISTICS`, `externalSource` `slack`|`gmail`, deduped by `externalId`; documents on the Slack message saved to S3 `intake-attachments/`) → appears in the `/intake` list. Status changes fire `notifySlackStatusChange` → **slack-status-notifier** posts back to Slack. **intake-reconcile** (every 20 min) caches each thread's latest reply onto the item and, once a labelled match shows the tender's load already exists, replies with the PRO in the tender's own thread and marks the item BUILT. Building a load from an Ivan item via Build load (a human click) prefills the drawer from the tender, attaches its stored rate con, and marks the item DONE.

### Loads are built by hand — never automatically
A tender arriving at `ivanloads@bcatcorp.com` must NEVER create a `Load`, and must
never place anything on the calendar. The intake path's only job is to capture the
documents and notify Slack; dispatch then builds the load manually once a human has
seen it. The one thing that may happen automatically is the load's details reaching
the owner-operator settlement.

A backfill script (`scripts/backfillIvanloadsLoads.mjs`, removed 2026-10-01) violated
this: it read the ivanloads mailbox and wrote `Load` rows with pickup and delivery
appointments, which put two shipments straight onto the calendar. The two rows it
created (`ivanloads-409670`, `ivanloads-98553`) were moved to Unscheduled rather than
deleted, so their driver and rate still reach the settlement. Do not reintroduce a
script of this shape — the manual step is the process, not a gap to be filled.

## Release-to-accounting gate (Step R)
`src/lib/releaseGate.ts` — `evaluateReleaseGate(input) → { ok, missing: ReleaseGateReason[] }` — is the one pure function both TMS routers import to decide whether an order may be billed. A voided or canceled shipment is refused outright; otherwise it accumulates every reason: not `DELIVERED`; a required document missing (`requiredDocTypesForRelease` — the customer's list, always `BOL` + `POD`, plus `RATE_CON` once an outside carrier leg is active; a SIGNED rate con counts); a required reference missing; no carrier/driver assigned; a CARRIER leg whose charges are not settled (no bill recorded); and any open exception (`Load.openIncidentCount` / `LoadCheckCall.incidentOpen`). `releaseGateError` / `releaseGateOf` carry the structured list to the browser (every message starts `Not ready for accounting`). Nothing overrides it, including factoring and batch invoicing.
