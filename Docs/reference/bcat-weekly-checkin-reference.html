What it does

Check-in history

True liquidity = cash − cards + receivables − payables. Watch it week over week: it should climb about as fast as group profit.

How the outlook is built. The current month starts from the latest check-in's cash. Its profit is the month-to-date figures scaled to a full month by how far through the month the check-in date is (a $6K MTD on the 15th becomes ~$12K). Every later month uses the run-rate profit for each center, plus any one-time items you've placed in that month.

Receivables and payables don't move the projection directly, because in a steady business they offset each other month to month. They feed true liquidity, which is the number that tells you whether a low bank balance is timing (liquidity high, cash low) or a real problem (both falling).
