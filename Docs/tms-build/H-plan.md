# Step H — Quotes & rates: customer quotes, carrier offers & lane history (dev)

## Architecture and scope

Planned by Claude Fable (`plan`, planning-only, as in A–G). Sources: `TMS_BUILD_BRIEF.md:85-86` (the one-line scope) and `:26-29` (steps follow spec §7, each usable on dev before the next); `MVMNT_TMS_SPEC.md` §6.6 sell-side quotes (:275), §6.5 Sourcing tab / buy-side offers (:266), §6.8 quote and offer vocabularies (:302, :305), §6.1 enums (:169, :178), §6.2 `Quote`/`QuoteGroup`/`QuoteAction` (:235), `Order`/`Load`/`LoadCarrier` quote fields (:197-199), customer quoting defaults (:210, :274) and org defaults (:237, :287), §6.3 actions (:241-242), §7 row H (:324) — **not** the superseded §5 row H "Reports" (:139); `TMS_DESIGN.md` §4.15 `LaneRateSample` (:336-340) and its GSIs (:394-395), §4.6 (:227-239), §4.7b `LoadBid` (:247-249), §4.16 `LINK` (:343, :556), Q11 (:572); `G-plan.md` and the Step G `BUILD_LOG` entry; every file cited below re-read in the working tree. There is **no Quote model** in `amplify/data/resource.ts` — the only `quote` hits are the legacy `sendVehicleQuoteEmail` mutation (`:2956-2969`) and the `QUOTED` customer lead stage (`:797-800`) — and nothing computes lane history (no `LaneHistory`/`avgRate`/`marketRate` anywhere; the only `rateHistory` is driver pay, `:494`). H adds **two new models** — **Quote** (one row per priced thing, `side SELL` = a customer quote, `side BUY` = a carrier offer, with its thread of events, its public-link token and its lines) and **LaneRateSample** (one row per delivered load, keyed by lane, the only thing the Historical Rates panel reads) — **no new Lambda**: `tmsShipmentActions` gains `quotes.ts` + `offers.ts` + `laneSamples.ts` behind a new `page-quotes` key, and `tracking-api` gains two public routes `GET /q/<token>` and `POST /q/<token>/respond`; additive fields on `Order`, `LoadCarrier`, `TmsSettings`, a `ShipmentNotice` kind; **zero GSIs on existing tables**; no new storage prefix (no quote PDF, judgement 9); a `/quotes` board + `/quotes/:id` detail, a `/rates` lane page, a public `/quote/:token` page outside the auth shell, a **Carrier offers** card on the shipment's Carrier tab, a **Lane history** panel on the quote form and the shipment's Financials, a **Quotes** tab on the customer profile, a settings card. New models `allow.authenticated().to(['read'])`, subscriptions disabled, Lambda-written (the `TrackingLink` shape, `resource.ts:1686-1689`). **Rate type Spot/Contract** (row H's fourth deliverable) already exists as `Order.rateType` (`resource.ts:1122`); H adds it to the quote, copies it on convert and splits lane history by it — it does not rebuild it.

**The status machine.** The canonical enum is `DRAFT · REQUESTED · QUOTED · SENT_COUNTER · RECEIVED_REPLY · RECEIVED_COUNTER · WON · LOST · PASS · CANCELED` — spec `:178` minus `CREATED` (the glossary already merges Draft/Created, `:302`), minus `SENT_REPLY` (a reply that carries no price is a message on the thread, not a rung — the only thing a rung may mean is "whose move is it and at what number"), minus `NOT_A_QUOTE` (the e-mail agent's reject bucket, step K). Buy-side labels map onto the same values (`:302`): Booked = WON, Not booked = LOST, Carrier pass = PASS, Broker pass = LOST with `lostReason WITHDRAWN`. One writer per transition, every one inside `tmsShipmentActions` except the two public responses. **Sell:** `SAVE_QUOTE` creates DRAFT (or REQUESTED with `intake: true` — a request we have not priced; the agent in K lands here, spec `:382`) and edits any DRAFT|REQUESTED in place under CAS on `version`; **DRAFT|REQUESTED → QUOTED happens only in `SEND_QUOTE`**, which mints the number (judgement 6), prices the lines through `computeFinancials` (`src/lib/shipmentCharges.ts:96`), issues a fresh `linkToken`, stamps `expiresAt`, and sends; **QUOTED|SENT_COUNTER → RECEIVED_REPLY|RECEIVED_COUNTER|LOST only in the public `respond`** (accept / counter / decline), in one `UpdateItem` conditioned `linkToken = :t AND #st IN (:quoted, :sentCounter)` that REMOVEs the token — one click per round, the second click is a 410 regardless of GSI lag; **RECEIVED_COUNTER → SENT_COUNTER only in `COUNTER_QUOTE`** (new price, new token, `rounds + 1`); **RECEIVED_COUNTER → RECEIVED_REPLY only in `ACCEPT_COUNTER`** (we take their number: the LINEHAUL line absorbs the difference, accessorials untouched, so `amountCents` still equals Σ lines); **→ WON only in `CONVERT_QUOTE`**, in the same transaction that writes the `Order` (judgement 4); open sent quotes past `expiresAt` → LOST `EXPIRED` only in `SWEEP_EXPIRED` (the board calls it once per open; the public Lambda writes the same thing when an expired link is opened, the `expire()` precedent `tracking-api/handler.ts:303-316`) — a quote that is merely past its hour is still shown as "expired" by the pure `quoteState()` before anyone writes it; `MARK_LOST {reason}` from any open rung; `RESTORE` LOST|CANCELED → QUOTED with **no token** — the customer gets a link again only through `SEND_QUOTE`, which is the only place a token is ever written; `CANCEL_QUOTE`; `PASS_QUOTE` (broker pass → PASS, sell side only). **Buy:** `REQUEST_OFFERS` creates one REQUESTED row per carrier under one `groupId` and sends; `LOG_OFFER` (a phone quote) creates QUOTED directly, no token; the public carrier `respond` moves REQUESTED → QUOTED (the carrier names a price), REQUESTED|SENT_COUNTER → RECEIVED_REPLY (accepts our number / book-now) or RECEIVED_COUNTER, or → PASS; `COUNTER_OFFER` → SENT_COUNTER; `REJECT_OFFER {reason}` → LOST; **→ WON only in `ACCEPT_OFFER`**, which runs the existing `BOOK_CARRIER` body (`handler.ts:2762`) with the offer's amount as the carrier rate and the full gate, then in the booking transaction stamps the offer WON and the siblings closed (priced → LOST `NO_REASON`… no: priced → LOST `TRUCK_NOT_AVAILABLE`, unpriced → CANCELED, spec `:302` "priced → Lost, unpriced → Canceled"), and writes `LoadCarrier.quoteId` (spec `:199`); a plain `BOOK_CARRIER` or `ASSIGN_ASSET` on a load that still has open offers CANCELs them in the same handler — a covered load never shows a live request. `Quote.quoteOpen` is the sparse `'OPEN'` carried from DRAFT through RECEIVED_COUNTER and REMOVEd (never nulled, `BUILD_LOG` `32d3eec`) on WON|LOST|PASS|CANCELED. There is **no `SET_QUOTE_STATUS`**, for G's reason (`G-plan.md:7`). **A lane sample** has one writer, `laneSamples.ts` inside `tmsShipmentActions`: a deterministic Put `id = lrs:<loadId>` when `SET_LOAD_STATUS` moves a load to DELIVERED or later **and a `LoadFinancials` row exists** (`resource.ts:1297`, design `:340`), re-Put by `repriceShipment` (`handler.ts:3249-3323`) and `updateCarrierCharges` (`:3405`) while the load is already DELIVERED+ (cost moves until `chargesFinalizedAt`, revenue until `frozenAt`, `:1310-1311`; after both the sample is final by construction). `tmsFinanceActions` writes no sample: an invoice freezes the numbers, it does not change them.

**Judgement calls.** (1) **Extend `tmsShipmentActions`; do not add `tmsQuoteActions`.** Every router is `defineFunction({ resourceGroupName: 'data' })` (`tms-shipment-actions/resource.ts`, and the same in every public API Lambda), so a new function lands **inside the capped data-stack template** as function + role + two policies + log group + env — the single most expensive thing H could add. Beyond bytes, a quote converts into a shipment and an offer books a carrier, and the code that does both is module-local: `chargeCodeRules` (`handler.ts:645-670`), `validateChargeInput` (`:673-711`), `defaultChargesFor` (`:745-766`), `pricingDefaultsOf` (`:769-773`), `priceFor` (`:1016-1045`), `repriceShipment` (`:3249-3323`), the `CREATE_SHIPMENT`/`SUBMIT_ORDER`/`CHECK_CREDIT` bodies (`resource.ts:3119-3122`) and `BOOK_CARRIER` with its gate (`:2762`; spec `:319`). A sibling would export all of that or copy it — F/G's `store.ts` argument again. Cost: the 4,400-line handler grows; mitigation is the finance precedent of per-domain modules (`bills.ts`, `apPayments.ts`) sharing the handler's helpers through explicit imports, and `authorize` (`:438-447`) gaining a `QUOTE_ACTIONS` set the way finance has `AP_ACTIONS` (`tms-finance-actions/handler.ts:145-172`). (2) **The public link is two routes on `tracking-api`, not a new Function URL.** `tracking-api` is already `FunctionUrlAuthType.NONE` with GET+POST and `PORTAL_ORIGINS` CORS (`backend.ts:1459-1465`), already published to the client as `trackingApiUrl` (`trackingClient.ts:13-18`), already has `activeLink` (verify kind + status + lazy expire, `tracking-api/handler.ts:289-301`), `countOpen` rate limiting (`:319-330`) and `isLinkToken` (`src/lib/trackingLink.ts:41-46`). Rejected: a `quote-link-api` (bytes, judgement 1); the `rate-con-sign-api` pattern, whose token sits on the row with **no index and a Scan per request** (`rate-con-sign-api/handler.ts:137-139, 140-164`, chosen there because `LoadCarrier` had spent its GSI slot, `resource.ts:1247-1250`); the dispute portal, which is public and token-less (`dispute-portal-api/handler.ts:166-189`) — never a precedent for a priced document. Also rejected: reusing `TrackingLink` with a new kind — `shipmentId` is required there (`resource.ts:1653`) and a sell-side quote has no shipment; writing a quote id into it is lying in a field. The token therefore lives on the Quote row as a **sparse, indexed `linkToken`** (the `OnboardingInvite.token` shape, `resource.ts:2566-2570`, read by Query not Scan), 24 random bytes base64url (`TOKEN_BYTES`, `tms-tracking-actions/handler.ts:136, 707, 980, 1430` — the same constant, lifted into `trackingLink.ts`), rotated per round and REMOVEd by the response. The quote's human id is separate (judgement 6), so a staff URL never carries the secret. (3) **One model, two sides; no `QuoteGroup`, no `QuoteAction` tables.** Spec `:235` is one `Quote` with `side BUY|SELL`; splitting it doubles the template cost for no behavioural gain — the fields differ by a handful (`customerId`/`contactEmail` vs `carrierId`/`carrierMc`/`bookNowRateCents`), the rungs are one enum, the link is one mechanism. `QuoteGroup` ("one email thread of quotes") is a `groupId` string on the row — a sell quote and its duplicates, or one request round on a load; `QuoteAction` (the audit of REQUESTED/QUOTED/COUNTERED/…) is `Quote.events: json[]` capped at 50 plus `AuditLog` (which every router already writes) — the thread is read with the quote, never alone. Design §4.7b `LoadBid` (`TMS_DESIGN.md:247-249`, GSI `:374`) was **never built** (no `LoadBid` in `resource.ts`); the BUY side of Quote supersedes it — a bid with one `accepted: boolean` cannot carry Requested/Countered/Passed. (4) **WON is written by the transaction that creates the Order, and a quote is not a load.** Design Q11 (`TMS_DESIGN.md:572`) asked whether a new load starts at `QUOTE`; H answers that nothing exists in `Shipment`/`Order`/`Load` until `CONVERT_QUOTE` — a quote the customer never takes must never inflate the board, the status GSIs (`resource.ts:1099-1102`) or the lane history. `CONVERT_QUOTE` runs the `CREATE_SHIPMENT` + `SAVE_ORDER_FORM` + `SUBMIT_ORDER` path with the quote's stops, equipment, references and lines (`repriceShipment` prices them exactly as the quote did — same `computeFinancials`, same rules, so the Order's `revenueCents` equals `Quote.amountCents` or the convert refuses), runs `CHECK_CREDIT` (spec `:275` "blocked by … credit limit, max days late"; `creditLimitCents`/`creditHoldFlag`, `resource.ts:783-784`), and in the final transaction writes `Order.quoteId/quoteNumber/quoteAmountCents/rateType`, Quote `{status WON, wonAt, shipmentId, orderId, REMOVE quoteOpen}` conditioned on the quote's `version`, and closes the group's siblings. A failure anywhere earlier leaves the quote where it was and the half-made shipment is deleted by the same guard `DELETE_SHIPMENT` uses. `REVERT_TO_QUOTE` (spec `:258`) is the only way back: single-order shipment, no ACTIVE|TONU assignment, no non-VOID invoice or bill (`assertNoInvoicedOrders`, `handler.ts:1528-1535`, extended in G) → soft-delete the shipment, quote WON → RECEIVED_REPLY ("accepted, not converted"), `shipmentId` cleared. (5) **A carrier's "accept" on the public link never books.** Spec `:266` says Accept "books + rejects the rest" — for the **staff** button. Booking runs the gate: allowed statuses, insurance vs cargo value, top spend, required fields, double-booking (spec `:319`), `DO_NOT_USE` refusals, a signed-in identity on the `AuditLog`. A public POST has none of that, so the carrier's acceptance lands RECEIVED_REPLY ("accepted our rate — book?") and a dispatcher clicks **Accept** → `ACCEPT_OFFER` → `BOOK_CARRIER`. `bookNowRateCents` on the request is the number the carrier may accept; it is not self-booking (spec `:305`'s "carrier self-booking" is deferred to the carrier-portal work in L). (6) **The quote id is the quote number.** Like F's invoice numbers, the number comes from a CAS counter on `TmsSettings` (`nextQuoteNumber`, format `quoteNumberFormat` default `Q{n:6}`, `DEFAULT_INVOICE_NUMBER_FORMAT` precedent), but here `id = number`: a Put with `attribute_not_exists(id)` doubles as the counter's race guard, and lookup by number is a `GetItem` — no `number` GSI (budget, below). DRAFT rows have no number yet? No: the number is minted at `SAVE_QUOTE` so a draft is linkable from day one — a draft that is never sent still consumes a number, as a voided invoice does. (7) **Lane history is a derived model, not a GSI on `Load`.** A lane query cannot be answered today: `Load` indexes `customerId/shipmentId/status/checkCallOpen` (`resource.ts:139-152`) and no origin/destination; `LoadFinancials` has **no** index (`:1316-1318`); `Shipment.pickupMonth+pickupAppt` (`:1102`) plus the city mirrors (`:1077-1078`) would scan a month and join financials by id for every lane lookup. `Load` and `Shipment` have each spent their one-GSI-per-update slot in earlier steps and a city key without a state is not a lane. `LaneRateSample` (design `:338`) is one row per load with the money already resolved from `LoadFinancials` (`customerRateCents/revenueCents/carrierRateCents/costCents`, `:1302-1305`), two GSIs on a **new** table (a new table may carry several indexes in one deploy — the one-per-update limit, `:1228`, is for existing tables), and `src/lib/laneStats.ts` is pure over one query. Rejected: writing `laneKey` on `Load` with a GSI (the slot), and a `load-stream-projector` Lambda (design `:514` — a stream handler is bytes and a second writer; the writes already pass through one Lambda). (8) **Customer and carrier see only their side.** The public page renders from `quotePublicView(quote, side)`: a customer sees stops, dates, equipment, the lines with labels and amounts, expiry, the brokerage/division name as `carrierRateCon.ts:130-139` prints it — never `targetBuyCents`, `marginBps`, `internalNotes`, a carrier name or a carrier price; a carrier sees origin/destination, dates, equipment, weight, commodity, our offer or book-now — never the customer's name or rate (the inverse of F/G's "never the customer's rate" rule on the remittance). The pure function is tested for both exclusions; the Lambda never builds a response any other way. (9) **No quote PDF in H.** The e-mail body and the public page are the quote; `GenerateDocumentType QUOTE` (spec `:176`) is a document step's pdf-lib job (I), when the rate-con/invoice layouts can be reused as a family. The e-mail reuses `src/lib/emailChrome.ts` and the SES CID-logo pattern of `vehicle-quote-emailer/handler.ts:1-16` — that is the "vehicle-quote emailer pattern" row H names (`:324`) — and nothing else from it.

**Order +**: `quoteId`, `quoteNumber`, `quoteAmountCents`, `quoteNotes` (spec `:197`); `rateType` unchanged (`:1122`). **LoadCarrier +**: `quoteId` (spec `:199`). **Customer**: nothing — `pricingDefaults`/`quotingDefaults` already exist (`:825-826`). **TmsSettings +**: `hoursToQuote` (24), `offerExpiryHours` (24, capped at the first pickup appt), `quoteNumberFormat` (`Q{n:6}`), `nextQuoteNumber`, `quoteEmailTemplate` / `counterEmailTemplate` / `offerRequestEmailTemplate` (each must contain `{link}`), `quoteTermsText`, `quoteBelowMinMarginAdminOnly` (true), `quoteOffersCarrierSeesBookNow` (true). **ShipmentNotice** `kind` **+ `CARRIER_OFFER`** (buy side only — a load exists; sell-side sends are recorded on the Quote row and the `AuditLog`, there is no shipment to hang a notice on). `TrackingLink`, `Load`, `Shipment`, `LoadFinancials`, `LoadCharge`, `Invoice`, `CarrierBill`, `Carrier`: unchanged. Storage: nothing new. **Secondary-index budget: no existing table spends its slot** — the five Quote indexes and two sample indexes are on new tables; offers-by-load is `loadId`+`quoteDate`, quotes-by-customer and the customer profile tab are `customerId`+`quoteDate`, the worklist is `quoteOpen`+`expiresAt` split by `side`/`status` in memory, closed history is by `quoteMonth`, the public lookup is `linkToken`, number lookup is the primary key. **Template budget.** The data stack synthesizes to 709,025 bytes against the 1,000,000-byte cap with `suppressTemplateIndentation` and `minimizePolicies` on. H adds **zero** Lambdas (judgement 1: a function in the `data` group is function + role + policies + log group + env, the most expensive unit) and **two** models with reads only and subscriptions disabled (fewest resolvers per model); additive attributes on existing models cost nothing in CloudFormation. Estimate from the Step F/G deltas as recorded in `BUILD_LOG`: ≈ 7–10 KB per model including its GSIs and the read resolvers, ≈ 1–2 KB for the new table ARNs and env on the `tmsShipmentActions` and `tracking-api` roles — **≤ 25,000 bytes spent, ≈ 735 KB after H, ≥ 265 KB headroom**. The build step measures the synthesized data template before the first schema change and after the last, records both numbers in the H `BUILD_LOG` entry, and if the post-H size exceeds 740,000 bytes drops the `quoteMonth` GSI (closed quotes are then listed per customer) before anything else.

## Pure libs (`src/lib/`, relative imports, Lambda-shared unless noted)

`quotes.ts`: `QUOTE_STATUSES`, `OPEN_QUOTE_STATUSES`, `LOST_REASONS`; `quoteState(quote, nowIso)` → `{rung, expired, whoseMove: US|THEM|NONE, canSend, canCounter, canAccept, canConvert, canRestore}` (the board, the detail page and the Lambda refusals all read this one function); `quoteExpiry(side, sentAtIso, customer, settings, pickupAppt)` → `sentAt + (customer.quotingDefaults.hoursToQuote ?? settings.hoursToQuote ?? 24) h`, BUY uses `offerExpiryHours` capped at the first pickup appt, minimum 1 h;
