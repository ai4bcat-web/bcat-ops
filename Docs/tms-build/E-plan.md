# Step E — Documents & delivery (dev)

## Architecture and scope

Planned by Claude Fable (`plan`, planning-only, as in A–D). Sources: `MVMNT_TMS_SPEC.md` §3.1, §3.5, §6.2 (`Document`, join rows with `attach`/`isSystemGenerated`), §6.5 (Documents tab, AR readiness), §7 row E; `TMS_DESIGN.md` §4.8 `LoadDocument`, §6; `TMS_BUILD_BRIEF.md` hard rules; the EScout survey (every file cited below re-read). E adds **two new models** — **ShipmentDocument** (one typed row per document attached to a shipment, in a customer / carrier / shipment section) and **ShipmentNotice** (every e-mail E tries to send, with its rendered text and SENT/NOT_SENT status) — a new `TrackingLink.kind` **CARRIER_UPLOAD** for the upload portal, additive fields on `Order`, `LoadCarrier`, `Shipment`, `TmsSettings`, the storage prefix `shipment-docs/*`, document actions on `tmsShipmentActions`, outside-world actions on `tmsTrackingActions`, three public routes on `tracking-api`, and one Load-stream consumer `shipment-notifier`. New models `allow.authenticated().to(['read'])`, subscriptions disabled, Lambda-written.

**Judgement calls.** (1) **One index, not a fifth store.** Four document stores exist (`CarrierDocument` carrier-docs/*, `PodDocument` pods/*, `DriverSubmission(Doc)` driver-docs/*, `Load.rateConfirmKey` rate-confirms/* — `amplify/data/resource.ts:1510, 1717, 555, 44`) and none knows about shipments. `ShipmentDocument` is the shipment's **index**: uploads and generated files put bytes under `shipment-docs/{shipmentId}/`; a driver-app POD or the customer's own rate con becomes a **pointer row** (`source DRIVER_APP|LEGACY`, `s3Key` = the existing key, `sourceRef` = the `DriverSubmissionDoc`/`Load` id; bytes never copied). The carrier rate con stays in `CarrierDocument` (`SEND_RATE_CON`, `tms-shipment-actions/handler.ts:2900-3010`; `rate-con-sign-api` SIGNED rows) and is **derived**, not mirrored: the tab lists it from `LoadCarrier.rateConDocumentId/rateConKey` and the gate counts `RATE_CON` from `rateConStatus SIGNED`. Extending `CarrierDocument` was rejected (PK is the carrier, index `carrierId+type`, a BOL has no carrier); `PodDocument` is JobsDone-only and client-locked. (2) **Generated PDFs stay in the browser.** The rate con is jsPDF in `src/lib/carrierRateCon.ts` and reaches the Lambda as base64 (`RateConPanel.tsx:164-167`); BOL and load summary take the same path (`buildBolPdf`, `buildLoadSummaryPdf` → `SAVE_GENERATED_DOCUMENT {pdfBase64}`). Cost: nothing server-side can generate a document unattended — accepted, because nothing in E needs that (delivery e-mails are text + links) and F's invoice PDF may still choose `pdf-lib` server-side as `TMS_DESIGN` Phase 4 planned. Text-only jsPDF files are tens of KB under the reused 10 MB `MAX_RATE_CON_BYTES`. Each generation is a **new version** (`version`, `supersedesId`; the old row becomes SUPERSEDED and keeps its bytes — "generated files are saved, not rewritten", spec §6.5). (3) **The gate gates Ready to invoice, never delivery.** Delivery is a fact about the truck (`loadStatusFromStopTimes`, projector); paperwork is a fact about billing. A new action `READY_TO_INVOICE` evaluates required documents and then performs the Order write `SET_AR_STATUS` does today; `SET_AR_STATUS` (`handler.ts:3775-3812`), `backfillLoadStatus` (`:3898`), `Load.readyToInvoice` and every POD-page write (`src/lib/podReviewClient.ts`) are untouched. Carrier-side missing documents (POD + carrier invoice before paying) come from the same evaluator with `scope 'CARRIER'`, stored for G; nothing in E blocks AP. (4) **Upload portal = D's token pattern on D's Lambda.** A `TrackingLink` whose id **is** the token (24 random bytes, GetItem, atomic `requestCount` cap — `tracking-api/handler.ts:296-300`) with `kind CARRIER_UPLOAD`, served by three new routes on `tracking-api` (already GET+POST, `PORTAL_ORIGINS`, outside `previewCallable`); the upload is the `tms-carrier-actions` presign-PUT + HeadObject-commit pattern (`handler.ts:1163-1250`). The onboarding-invite pattern finds tokens by Scan and was rejected. (5) **Carrier uploads are reviewed before they count**, as driver PODs are (`DriverSubmission.podReviewStatus`): a portal upload lands `reviewStatus PENDING` and satisfies nothing until staff accept it — which is also what keeps the public role from ever writing `Order`. (6) **Notifications are rows first, e-mails second.** Every send writes a `ShipmentNotice` with the rendered subject/body, then tries SES and records SENT or NOT_SENT with the error. On dev that row *is* the deliverable (Copy body / Copy link) — D's `emailStatus 'NOT_SENT'` honesty (`tms-tracking-actions/handler.ts:941-958`).

**Corrections to the brief/spec from the code.** `Customer.requiredDocsForInvoice` and `TmsSettings.defaultRequiredDocs` are **free text** (comma/line-split — `CustomerOrgDefaults.tsx:28-34`, `useCustomerForm.ts:140,248`; the directory Lambda checks length only), so no vocabulary exists to gate on; E introduces one, normalises at read time (`normalizeRequiredDocTypes`: upper-case, aliases "Rate confirmation"→RATE_CON, "Proof of delivery"→POD, unknown strings kept and reported as `unrecognised`) and replaces both editors with a typed picker. "Driver-app PODs land as carrier documents automatically" cannot be a server hook: POD approval is a **client-side AppSync write** (`podReviewClient.saveReview`) and `DriverSubmission` has no `loadId` index (`resource.ts:609-613`); "automatic" is the review panel firing `ATTACH_DRIVER_DOC` after a successful approve on a shipment-linked load, plus the backfill. `podReviewStatus` stays the own-fleet truth; an attached driver POD is ACCEPTED by construction. "Typed documents with expiry" is a carrier-packet property (`CarrierDocument.expiresAt`); shipment documents do not expire. Row E's "invoice" document is F's; "delivery/routing alerts" are the milestone e-mails, not PDFs. `tmsTrackingActions` *is* in `previewCallable` (`backend.ts:2225`), contrary to D-plan's text — harmless, SES is denied there. Dev denies `ses:*` on every Lambda (`backend.ts:2408`): no E e-mail can be proven end-to-end on dev; every send degrades to a NOT_SENT notice with copyable text, and the stream consumer is created **enabled** on dev (like the projector, `backend.ts:2019`) because its only external call is the SES one dev refuses. **Deferred:** invoice PDF/e-mail and `INVOICE_SENT` notices (F); AP use of `LoadCarrier.docsMissing` and the carrier-invoice audit (G); Trucker Tools document capture (J); `APPT_SCHEDULED` notices (not a status transition; the Appts board owns appointments); SMS delivery notices (Twilio unconfigured on dev); carrier rating aggregates on the profile (H/I read `LoadCarrier.ratingStars`); OCR of carrier uploads (`pod-actions/podCheck.ts` is driver-flow specific).

## Schema (`amplify/data/resource.ts`)

**ShipmentDocument** (new; id `sdoc-<uuid>`, or deterministic `drv:<submissionDocId>` / `rck:<loadId>` for pointer rows so a retry or backfill re-run cannot list a file twice): `shipmentId!`, `orderId` (set → **Customer** section), `loadId`, `loadCarrierId` (set → **Carrier** section; neither → Shipment section), `stopId`, `type!` BOL·POD·RATE_CON·CUSTOMER_RATE_CON·LOAD_SUMMARY·LUMPER_RECEIPT·WEIGHT_TICKET·TEMP_LOG·INSPECTION_REPORT·CUSTOMS·PACKING_LIST·CARRIER_INVOICE·INVOICE·PHOTO·OTHER, `name!`, `s3Key!`, `contentType!`, `sizeBytes!`, `pages`, `source!` UPLOAD·CARRIER_PORTAL·GENERATED·DRIVER_APP·LEGACY, `sourceRef`, `audience!` CUSTOMER·CARRIER·ALL·INTERNAL (who may receive it outside; per-type defaults in `shipmentDocuments.ts`), `status!` ACTIVE·SUPERSEDED·VOID, `version!` (per shipment + type + loadId), `supersedesId`, `reviewStatus` PENDING·ACCEPTED·REJECTED (sparse: only CARRIER_PORTAL rows carry PENDING), `reviewedAt/By`, `reviewNote`, `uploadLinkId`, `voidedAt/By`, `voidReason`, `uploadedBy!` (staff email · `link:<token8>` · `driver:<id>` · `migration:<script>`), `createdBy!`. GSIs `shipmentId`+`createdAt`, `reviewStatus`+`createdAt`. **ShipmentNotice** (new; id `ms:<shipmentId>:<milestone>` for milestone rows, `pod:<loadCarrierId>` for the first POD request, `ntc-<uuid>` for manual sends): `shipmentId!`, `loadId`, `loadCarrierId`, `kind!` MILESTONE·POD_REQUEST·DOCUMENTS, `milestone` (the `CustomerNotification` vocabulary, `src/types/tms.ts:37-45`), `channel!` EMAIL·COPY, `to!` (comma-joined), `cc`, `subject!`, `body!` (≤20 000 chars — the copyable artifact), `attachments: json` `{documentId, name, sizeBytes}[]`, `status!` SENT·NOT_SENT·FAILED·COPY, `error`, `messageId`, `sentAt`, `trackingLinkId`, `createdBy!`. GSI `shipmentId`+`createdAt`.

**TrackingLink** `kind` **+ `CARRIER_UPLOAD`** (additive enum value), **+** `uploadCount`, `lastUploadAt`, `neededTypes: string[]`. **Order +**: `docsMissing: string[]` (cached by the Lambda after every document write; `[]` = complete), `docsRequired: string[]`, `docsCheckedAt`, `docsOverrideAt/By`, `docsOverrideNote`. **LoadCarrier +**: `uploadLinkId` (the ACTIVE CARRIER_UPLOAD link), `podRequestedAt`, `docsMissing: string[]` (carrier scope, for G), `ratingStars` (1–5), `ratingNote`, `ratedAt/By`. **Shipment +**: `deliveredAt`, `lastNoticeAt`, `lastNoticeStatus`. **TmsSettings +**: `milestoneNoticesEnabled` (true), `podRequestEnabled` (true), `requiredCarrierUploadTypes: string[]` (['POD','CARRIER_INVOICE']), `uploadLinkExpiryDays` (30, from delivery), `milestoneEmailTemplate`, `podRequestEmailTemplate` (must contain `{url}`), `bolTerms`, `documentDeleteAdminOnly` (true). Storage (`amplify/storage/resource.ts`): `shipment-docs/*` → authenticated + `STAFF_GROUPS` **read only** (uploads are Lambda-presigned PUTs; no browser write, no delete — VOID keeps the file as `DELETE_DOCUMENT` does). **Secondary-index budget:** one new GSI per *existing* table per deploy. E adds **zero** GSIs to Load, Shipment, Order, LoadCarrier, Customer, TrackingLink or TmsSettings: documents-by-shipment and the review worklist ride the two indexes on the new ShipmentDocument table, notices ride ShipmentNotice's own, the upload token is a primary key, and the board's Docs column reads the cached `Order.docsMissing` that `listOrderByShipmentId` already returns. Every existing table's slot stays free for F.

## Lambdas

**`tmsShipmentActions` +** (document rows and the AR gate belong to the shipment's owner router; new module `tms-shipment-actions/documents.ts`, `handler.ts` only dispatches). `PREPARE_DOCUMENT_UPLOAD {shipmentId, type, name, contentType, sizeBytes}` → `{uploadUrl, s3Key, documentId}` (presigned PUT pinned to type + length, 300 s; shipment exists and not deleted; writes nothing). `COMMIT_DOCUMENT {shipmentId, documentId, s3Key, type, name, contentType, sizeBytes, orderId?, loadId?, loadCarrierId?, stopId?, audience?}` — refuses a key outside `shipment-docs/{shipmentId}/`, HeadObject mismatch (NotFound → the retryable "has not landed" error), ids not in the bundle; idempotent on `s3Key`; writes the ACTIVE row (`source UPLOAD`, `version = nextVersion`) and re-caches `Order.docsMissing` (and `LoadCarrier.docsMissing` for carrier-section rows) in one transaction; AuditLog `SHIPMENT_DOCUMENT`. `SAVE_GENERATED_DOCUMENT {shipmentId, type BOL|LOAD_SUMMARY|RATE_CON, loadId?, loadCarrierId?, pdfBase64, filename}` — refuses DRAFT and CANCELED, empty or > 10 MB bytes, RATE_CON without an ACTIVE assignment; PutObject under `generatedKey`, new version, the previous ACTIVE row of that type/load → SUPERSEDED, audience from `DEFAULT_AUDIENCE`. `ATTACH_DRIVER_DOC {shipmentId, loadId, submissionDocId, type POD|PHOTO|OTHER}` — reads the `DriverSubmissionDoc` and its `DriverSubmission`; refuses when the submission's `loadId` ≠ `loadId`, a POD whose `podReviewStatus` is NEEDS_REPLACEMENT, a load outside the shipment; conditional Put of `drv:<submissionDocId>` (`s3Key = combinedPodKey ?? enhancedKey ?? s3Key`, `source DRIVER_APP`, `sourceRef submissionId`, `audience ALL`); already attached → returns the row. `REVIEW_DOCUMENT {documentId, decision ACCEPT|REJECT, type?, audience?, note?}` — PENDING rows only; ACCEPT may retype within `CARRIER_UPLOADABLE_TYPES`; recaches. `UPDATE_DOCUMENT {documentId, name?, type?, audience?, orderId?, loadCarrierId?}` — ACTIVE non-pointer rows; generated rows may change audience only. `VOID_DOCUMENT {documentId, reason}` — ADMIN, or the uploader unless `documentDeleteAdminOnly`; row → VOID, file kept, recache. `READY_TO_INVOICE {orderId, expectedUpdatedAt, override?, overrideNote?}` — refuses: shipment not DELIVERED and `invoiceEarly` false (SET_AR_STATUS's rule, repeated here so that action stays untouched), `arStatus` not DOCS_NEEDED, `missing.length > 0` unless `override` (ADMIN/owner, note required) — the error lists the missing labels; writes `arStatus UNBILLED`, `arStatusChangedAt`, `readyToInvoiceAt`, `docsMissing/docsCheckedAt`, `docsOverride*`, `version + 1`; AuditLog `ORDER READY_TO_INVOICE {missing, override}`. `RATE_CARRIER {loadCarrierId, stars 1–5, note?}` — `kind CARRIER`, load DELIVERED or later. Grants: ShipmentDocument Put/Update/Query(index), Order + LoadCarrier Update, DriverSubmission + DriverSubmissionDoc Get, `grantReadWrite(shipmentActionsFn, 'shipment-docs/*')`; env `SHIPMENT_DOCUMENT_TABLE_NAME`, `DRIVER_SUBMISSION_TABLE_NAME`, `DRIVER_SUBMISSION_DOC_TABLE_NAME`. `readBundle` gains the ShipmentDocument query; `ShipmentBundle` + `documents`.
