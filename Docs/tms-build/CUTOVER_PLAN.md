## 7. Rollback path: decision points, time limits, limits of what can be undone

**The core fact: after the merge deploy succeeds there is no quick infrastructure rollback.** Reverting main would have CloudFormation delete 49 tables `[INFERENCE]` (the 3 Load GSIs are no longer part of the merge once P3 is done), and table deletion policy on the Amplify tables is unverified (`DeletionProtectionEnabled=False` on dev and prod). After F11 the practical rollback is a **business rollback** (reopen Aljex) plus forward fixes, with data restores for individual tables. Hence the gates before F11 carry the real risk control.
