# Step G — AP: carrier bills & payments (dev)

## Architecture and scope

Planned by Claude Fable (`plan`, planning-only, as in A–F). Sources: `MVMNT_TMS_SPEC.md` §6.2 (`Invoice` as BILL, `PaymentGroup`, `CreditMemo`, lines 225-226), §6.5 AP ladder (:270), §6.6 Bills / AP Payments / AP Credits (:278), §6.8 AP status (:299), §7 row G (:323); `TMS_DESIGN.md` §4.13 `CarrierBill`, §4.14, §5, §6; `TMS_BUILD_BRIEF.md` hard rules; `F-plan.md` and the Step F `BUILD_LOG` entry; every file cited below re-read in the working tree. G adds **two new models** — **CarrierBill** (one recorded bill for one carrier assignment: line snapshot, quick-pay fee, payee and remit-to snapshot, balance) and **ApPayment** (every payment sent, AP credit and reversal, with its allocations) — **no new router**: `tmsFinanceActions` gains `bills.ts` + `apPayments.ts` behind a new `carrierPay` page key; additive fields on `LoadCarrier`, `TmsSettings`, `ShipmentNotice`; **one GSI on `LoadCarrier`**; the storage prefix `remittances/*`; an `/ap` page; an AP card on the shipment's Carrier tab; an AP tab on the carrier profile; a settings card. New models `allow.authenticated().to(['read'])`, subscriptions disabled, Lambda-written.

**The status machine.** `LoadCarrier.apStatus` already carries the ladder (`resource.ts:1212-1214`) and `LoadCarrier.invoiceId` is the slot for the bill (`:1214`). Today **everything** writes AWAITING_INVOICE and nothing else: `BOOK_CARRIER` (`tms-shipment-actions/handler.ts:2676`), the rebook in `ASSIGN_CARRIER` (`:2289`), `DUPLICATE_SHIPMENT` (`:1893`), and — the first correction — `ASSIGN_ASSET` (`:2199`) and the legacy backfill (`:4084`), which stamp it on **own-fleet** rows with `costCents: 0`. No screen reads it (grep `apStatus` in `src/features`: nothing). G adds four transitions and one writer per money-moving step: **AWAITING_INVOICE → IN_REVIEW happens only inside `RECORD_BILL`**, in the `TransactWriteItems` that puts the `CarrierBill` row and stamps the assignment's `chargesFinalizedAt` (`resource.ts:1208` — the field exists, `SET_QUICK_PAY` already refuses on it, `handler.ts:2766-2768`, and **nothing writes it**; `DUPLICATE_SHIPMENT` only nulls it, `:1890`); **IN_REVIEW → TO_BE_PAID only inside `APPROVE_BILL`** behind the gate below (or in the same `RECORD_BILL` transaction with `approve: true`, which is how a brokerage that turned the review stage off works — `TmsSettings.apInReviewStage` is a label string nobody reads, `resource.ts:955`, the `arUnbilledStage` twin F found); **TO_BE_PAID → PAID only inside `RECORD_AP_PAYMENT`** when the transaction leaves `CarrierBill.balanceCents` at 0 (a partial leaves TO_BE_PAID with the bill PARTIAL); **PAID → TO_BE_PAID only inside `REVERSE_AP_PAYMENT`**. `VOID_BILL` is the only way back (IN_REVIEW/TO_BE_PAID → AWAITING_INVOICE, `invoiceId` cleared, `chargesFinalizedAt` REMOVEd, bill kept VOID, refused while `paidCents > 0`). There is **no `SET_AP_STATUS`**: F had to add a refusal to a hand-set action (`handler.ts:3731-3748`); G never builds one. `Load.status INVOICING` (`resource.ts:93`) is not written by G — the load status is the movement, the AP rung lives on the assignment. A **carrier bill** is an immutable snapshot: the assignment's CARRIER `LoadCharge` rows (`resource.ts:1304` — `carrierOwnCostCents` is the filter, `handler.ts:2472-2476`), `grossCents`, `quickPayFeeCents` copied from the row (`resource.ts:1220-1223`), `netCents`, payee and remit-to taken from the carrier's preferred `CarrierPaymentMethod` (`isPreferred` `:1772`, `recipientType CARRIER|FACTOR` `:1762`, never `bankDetailsKey`), terms and due date. Nothing re-reads them; a mistake is voided and re-recorded. **An AP payment** is a row with `applications[{billId, amountCents}]` and `creditApplications[{paymentId, amountCents}]`; **an AP credit** (a claim, a TONU adjustment, an advance we are owed back) is an `ApPayment` of kind CREDIT_MEMO spent only through a later payment; **a reversal** restores balances and marks the original, never edits it. **AP aging** is a pure function over the open bills read from one sparse index.

**Judgement calls.** (1) **Extend `tms-finance-actions`; do not extend `vendor-ap-actions`; do not add a sibling.** `vendor-ap-actions` is a two-rung queue (`VendorPayable.status` NEED_TO_PAY·DONE, `resource.ts:1948`) with one `amount` and no allocations; its `REOPEN` erases the payment fields in place (`vendor-ap-actions/handler.ts:687-688`) — a mutable ledger, the opposite of F's rule; its methods are display strings `'ACH','Check','Credit card'` (`:28`); its gate is `page-vendorAp` (`:145-155`); it has no payee entity, no partial pay, no credit, no reversal, no remittance. `TMS_DESIGN` §4.13's `SEND_CARRIER_BILL` hand-off (`TMS_DESIGN.md:326`) is rejected for the same reason: paying there would close a bill outside the transaction that writes the payment. A sibling `tms-ap-actions` would copy `store.ts` — table names, identity, audit, `updateTx`/`putTx`/`unsetCondition`/`NULL_TYPE_VALUE` (`tms-finance-actions/store.ts:202-276`), S3, the notice log — or force it into `_shared` for no isolation gain; the only thing that differs is one page-key check (`handler.ts:86-88`), and `TMS_DESIGN` §6 named this router `invoicing / carrierPay` from the start (`TMS_DESIGN.md:421`). Cost: `spendCreditTx` is pinned to `AR_PAYMENT_TABLE_NAME` (`store.ts:232-235`) and `ledgerWrites` to the Invoice/Order tables (`payments.ts:184-262`) — the first gains a table argument, the second stays AR-only and AP gets its own `apLedgerWrites` (the per-document update is a different table with different conditions). `postOnce` and `paymentId` (`payments.ts:144-148, 166-181`) move to `store.ts` unchanged. (2) **One planner, two vocabularies.** `planPayment` (`src/lib/paymentApply.ts:131-290`) is exactly AP's arithmetic — application ≤ balance, credit ≤ `unappliedCents`, Σ ≤ cash + credits, VOID refused, remainder unapplied — but its types say `customerId`/`invoiceId` and its messages say "customer" and "invoice" (`:170, 215, 219`). A copy is rejected; so is lying in a type. The planner's own types rename to `partyId`/`documentId` (`PlanInvoice` → `PlanDocument`, `PlanCredit.partyId`) with a `vocabulary: {party, document}` argument used only in messages; `payments.ts:78-98`, `ReceivePaymentDialog` and `paymentApply.test.ts` take the mechanical rename and F's tests pass as they are. What genuinely differs on the pay side is **outside** the planner: (a) an AP PAYMENT with `unappliedCents > 0` is refused — we do not send money we cannot allocate (MVMNT: Paid when allocations + credits cover the amount due, spec :278); only a CREDIT_MEMO carries unapplied money; (b) the amount a bill is settled for is its `netCents` — gross less the quick-pay fee already cached on the row — so the fee is applied once, at RECORD_BILL, never by the planner or the dialog; (c) a remittance advice is a document over one payment and its N bills, rendered by `remittancePdf.ts` (pdf-lib, isomorphic, the `invoicePdf.ts` precedent) and sent through `deliverNotice`; (d) the row's date is the day the money left, `paidAt`, and the method set is the carrier-method enum (`resource.ts:1760`) — no FACTOR method: paying a factor is still an ACH. (3) **Payee = carrier; the factor is where the money goes, not who is owed.** `CarrierPaymentMethod.recipientType FACTOR` + `factorName` (`:1762-1763`) and `Carrier.factorPaymentMethodId` (`:1708`) say *where* to remit; there is no factor entity to key a payment on, and inventing one is step L's billing/payee work. One payment per carrier; its remit-to snapshot names the factor; the quick-pay fee is **zero** when the payee is a factor (spec :278). A factor-level payee covering many carriers is deferred (decision below). (4) **The assignment's cost is finalized at RECORD_BILL, not at the customer's invoice.** `GENERATE_INVOICE` freezes every load's `LoadFinancials` (`invoices.ts:390-397`) and `RECALC_CHARGES` refuses wholesale after `frozenAt` (`handler.ts:3191-3194`), rewriting every line in the shipment when it does run (`:3136-3138`). So MVMNT's "Update charges" at Record bill — a lumper on the carrier's invoice the customer was never billed — is impossible today once the customer is invoiced. G adds one shipment action, **`UPDATE_CARRIER_CHARGES {loadCarrierId, charges[]}`**: CARRIER lines of that one assignment only, recomputes `LoadFinancials` cost/profit/margin and the row's cached pay through the existing `repriceShipment` totals, refused after `chargesFinalizedAt` and never touching a CUSTOMER line — `frozenAt` keeps meaning "the invoice's lines will not move". `repriceShipment` itself gains one refusal: a CARRIER line whose assignment carries `chargesFinalizedAt` may not change or disappear. (5) **The AP worklist is one sparse index on `LoadCarrier`.** "Awaiting invoice" rows have no bill row yet, so no bill index can list them; `loadCarriersByCarrierId` is per carrier; `loadsByStatus` DELIVERED grows forever (`resource.ts:146`). `LoadCarrier.apOpen` is the sparse `'OPEN'`, carried on **CARRIER-kind** rows that are ACTIVE or TONU from booking until PAID, REMOVEd (never nulled — `BUILD_LOG` `32d3eec`) on PAID and on BOUNCE (a bounced carrier is owed nothing; a TONU'd one is, `handler.ts:2386-2393`). One query, split into the three tabs by `apStatus` in memory; the open set is bounded by brokered freight not yet paid. (6) **Own-fleet assignments never enter AP.** The fork is `LoadCarrier.kind` (`resource.ts:1174`) / `Load.assignmentType` (`:102`), decided at `ASSIGN_ASSET` (`handler.ts:2185, 2222`) and at `BOOK_CARRIER` (`:2648, 2721`). Settlements already read own-fleet loads from the legacy mirrors — `ownerOpTripsFor` wants `deliveryDriverId`, `deliveryAppt`, `rate` (`src/lib/ownerOperatorTrips.ts:118-141`) — which `ASSIGN_ASSET` writes through `deriveLegacyFields(stops)` (`handler.ts:2208-2217`) and `RECALC_CHARGES` keeps mirrored (`Load.rate` = order revenue, `:3213-3218`). G changes nothing in `driverPay.ts`, box-truck or owner-operator hooks. It (a) stops `ASSIGN_ASSET` and the backfill writing `apStatus` on OWN_ASSET rows and REMOVEs it in the migration, (b) never writes `apOpen` on them, (c) refuses `RECORD_BILL` on `kind !== 'CARRIER'` ("paid through driver settlements"), and (d) the AP card on an own-asset leg says exactly that and links to the driver's settlement period. (7) **Bills, credits and reversals are one model**, as F (5). (8) **A bill's amount is the lines, and a variance blocks approval.** `billedCents` (what the carrier's invoice says) is typed at RECORD_BILL; `grossCents` is Σ lines. They may differ while IN_REVIEW; `APPROVE_BILL` refuses until they agree — fix the lines (`UPDATE_CARRIER_CHARGES`), or void. Approving "pay the carrier's number" without the lines agreeing would make cost ≠ financials, which is the drift the whole cached-totals design exists to prevent. (9) **The remittance PDF is rendered server-side with pdf-lib**, F (3) verbatim: the e-mail must attach the bytes it stored, and `invoicePdf.ts` already lays out a table.

**Corrections to the brief/spec from the code.** The spec's gate for paying a carrier is mostly data already: the carrier-scope paperwork gate exists (`evaluateDocGate` scope CARRIER, `src/lib/shipmentDocuments.ts:424-446`, default required `['POD','CARRIER_INVOICE']` `:138-141`) and is cached on `LoadCarrier.docsMissing` (`resource.ts:1257-1259`, written by `recacheDocGates`, `documents.ts:244-255`) — but the cache skips every non-ACTIVE row (`documents.ts:245`), so a **TONU'd assignment's gate is stale forever**; G evaluates the gate live at approval and treats the cache as the board's colour only. The signed rate con is a status, not a document: `RATE_CON` counts as present when `rateConStatus === 'SIGNED'` (`shipmentDocuments.ts:383`); today nothing blocks on it — dispatch only adds the `RATE_CON_NOT_SIGNED` warning (`handler.ts:3017`). G makes an unsigned rate con an approval **block** for non-admins and an acknowledgeable warning for admins, recorded on the bill like `bookingWarnings` (`resource.ts:1245`). The carrier's own invoice arrives through E's portal as a `ShipmentDocument` `source CARRIER_PORTAL`, `reviewStatus PENDING` (`tracking-api/handler.ts:728-731`); it satisfies nothing until accepted on `/documents` (`navItems.ts:55`), and `RECORD_BILL` links the accepted row as `documentId` and prefills `billNumber`/`billedCents` from nothing — there is no extraction for carrier invoices (that is step K's AP agent). `CarrierPaymentEditor.tsx:49` already tells the user "AP cannot pay this carrier until one is added"; G makes the missing method a refusal at approval, not at payment. Quick pay is complete on the write side: `SET_QUICK_PAY` caches days/bps/cents (`handler.ts:2756-2800`), `repriceShipment` re-derives the fee when the rate moves (`:3166-3170`), the rate con prints fee and net (`carrierRateCon.ts:312-320`) — and the comment at `:2752-2754` says "the money moves in step G". It does, once: `netCents = grossCents − quickPayFeeCents`, and the bill's `termsDays` is `quickPayDays` when quick pay is on (paying early **is** the term), else `LoadCarrier.paymentTermsDays ?? Carrier.paymentTermsDays ?? TmsSettings.defaultCarrierTermsDays ?? 30` (`resource.ts:1224, 1705, 965`). A rebook after a TONU leaves **two payable assignments** on one load (`handler.ts:2386-2393` keeps the TONU'd lines); each gets its own bill, and `carrierOwnCostCents` already ignores the other's lines. `DynamoDB allows one new GSI per table update` (`resource.ts:1228`): the `LoadCarrier` index is the only one G adds to an existing table. SES is denied on dev (`backend.ts:2699`): every remittance lands NOT_SENT with copyable text and 1-hour downloads, as F's invoices did (`BUILD_LOG` Step F). The `tmsFinanceActions` grants already cover LoadCarrier, LoadFinancials and LoadCharge for Get/Update (`backend.ts:2028-2054`); G adds the two new tables, Carrier / CarrierPaymentMethod / CarrierContact reads and `remittances/*`. **Deferred:** vended services / vendor AP ladder on a shipment (spec :292 — stays on `/vendor-ap`; I or L); check printing; a factor payee entity and factor-level remittance (L billing/payees); carrier-invoice e-mail ingestion (K); QuickBooks bill sync (J); AP on `Load.status INVOICING` (never); multi-currency; commissions on cost (I).
